My Xbox 360 renders

I made these renders in late August of 2026 because I wanted to try making a complex model with decently good topology plus I was feeling slightly nostalgic while looking at my Xbox 360 controller. This project also took me about a week to finish since I had to make many background assets for this. I also usually only make my own assets because I want to look at my renders and think to my self "I made this all by myself", it's just a good feeling to have.
