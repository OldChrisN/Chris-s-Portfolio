My SLS renders

I made this project in January of 2026 and it was my first project where my renders had higher poly and more detailed models, I decided to make it after watching the 2026 Donut Blender tutorial by the creator Blender Guru. Around this time I was also excited about the Artemis 2 rocket launch so I thought it would be great to have a project dedicated to showing off how strong the SLS looked. For the modelling it was very easy I mainly had a cylinder where I subdivided it and used loop cuts to make the edges smoother.
