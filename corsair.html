My F4U Corsair renders

I made this render right after the SLS render so I could see that if I only made a model due to it having a basic cylinder shape or if I actually could apply it to slightly more complex shapes like the F4U. I also wanted to make something that had a similar theme to Top Gun cause I thought it would look cool. Making the F4U Corsair was actually easier than it looked, I just had to adapt the shape of cylinder by giving it decent topology and using proportional editting to move around vertices in my model.
