My Boy in the Black Suit renders

I made these four renders in 2026 for a school project because I really liked Jason Reynold's The Boy in the Black Suit and I felt it deserved a beautiful presentation, so I made a Casket and Tombstone which represented the main character Matt's losses and the job he has to work at a funeral home and made the renders of the book and a repast for cinematic looking scenes that still related to my project. I also made these renders to make other models besides hard surfaced aerospaace related stuff.
